FAQ

Before you message me.

Do I need an account to contact you? +

No. You can submit the project form directly. There is no client account or signup required.

Do you work with clients remotely? +

Yes. Projects can be handled remotely regardless of where the client is located.

Can you redesign my existing website? +

Yes. Send the existing website URL and explain what isn't working. We can determine whether a redesign or rebuild makes more sense.

Can you help after the website launches? +

Yes. Maintenance, updates and additional improvements can be discussed as part of the project or separately.

How do I start? +

Fill out the form above with as much detail as you currently have. You don't need to have everything figured out before contacting me.
